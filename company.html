Who we are

vet-eye designs and manufactures high-end ultrasound scanners for veterinary medicine. Our team of more than 45 people is based in Lublin, Poland, and our equipment is at work in over 10,000 clinics every day.

What we stand for

We lead with science and back research that improves the health and wellbeing of animals.

We stand with veterinarians in their stewardship of animal health.

We welcome new ideas and embrace change.
